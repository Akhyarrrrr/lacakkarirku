# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Job seekers managing several role targets, CV variants, applications, and follow-up commitments.

## Product Purpose

Turn scattered job links and CV versions into a trustworthy, reviewable application workflow.

## Brand Personality

Supportive, transparent, practical.

## Anti-references

Ambiguous apply actions, opaque AI scores, stale job feeds, and dashboards that demand constant checking.

## Design Principles

- Separate opening a vacancy from recording an application.
- Show freshness and uncertainty wherever jobs or AI are involved.
- Preserve the CV version used for every decision.
- Bring follow-ups back to the user at the right time.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, readable contrast, labeled controls, keyboard-safe navigation, and reduced motion.
